A crisp, refreshing Berliner Weisse brewed for those moments when you find a little more in the tank than you thought.

Built on a simple foundation of wheat and pilsner malt, Second Wind delivers a bright, gentle tartness with a clean finish and just enough character to keep you moving. Light, refreshing, and endlessly drinkable—perfect for a mid-ride reset, a hot Texas afternoon, or any time you need that extra push to keep going.

Sometimes the trail gives you exactly what you need. Sometimes you just need a second wind.
